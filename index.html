Magic War for Android

Effective November 24, 2026 · com.liangtech.magicwar

Data on your device

Magic War reads your device language to choose the initial display language. It stores your game settings, progress, and diagnostic logs on your device to remember your choices, restore your progress, and diagnose technical problems on the device. You can remove these files by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the game.

Collection and sharing

The game does not require an account and does not send your settings, progress, or local diagnostic logs to the developer or any third party. The current Android version does not include in-game advertising, third-party analytics, or in-app purchases.

Google Play may handle information about downloads, testing enrollment, and device diagnostics as part of its own services. Google describes that processing in its privacy policy.

Email and test participation

If you email the developer or provide an email address to join a test, your address and any information you choose to send are used to respond to you or manage test access in Google Play. Email communications are handled through our email provider. Access to this information is limited to people handling your request or test access. We keep it for as long as needed for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request removal from the test and deletion of information you provided.
